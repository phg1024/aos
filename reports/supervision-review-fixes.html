Supervision: Two Fixes After the Chapter 7 Review

An independent review of Chapter 7 found two defects in v0.1.3 (ae7bea8). Both are fixed on local main, after the tag.

  • The restart bound: a short window turned the restart intensity off. Every valid policy now also has a total.
  • Interrupted calls: a failed handover in Recover could lose an interrupted call. The note and the transfers now walk the process chain.

Result:

  • go vet, go test ./... and the audit probes pass.
  • Eval's three ca78ad9 trials replay identically (31/31, 8/8, 9/9).

1. A Window Is a Rate, Not a Bound

Exceeded counted only the restarts within within. A window shorter than one restart cycle never fills, so max never triggers. A large backoff can keep a long window from filling too, as can a slow agent.

The choice:

  • total is the bound: the most restarts a process chain may have in all, whatever the timing. It defaults to 10, or max if that is larger. It is counted from the same recorded supervisor.restart events as the window.
  • Why not a window floor alone: no floor can outlast a restart cycle of unknown length, because a model step can take any time.
  • The floor still exists: a window must be at least 1s, and longer than the backoffs of max restarts. Otherwise max could never be reached, which makes it dead configuration.
  • A huge max: capped. max and total are at most 100 (supervisor.MaxRestarts), and total cannot be below max. An agent restarted a hundred times is not failing transiently.
  • Validation names the agent, through agent worker: ….

2. A Failed Handover Lost the Interrupted Call

In Recover, only Kernel.Supersede is atomic. The mail transfer, the lease transfer and noteInterrupted follow it as separate writes. If one of them failed, the successor exited retryably before it was told. The predecessor, now superseded, could never be recovered again. Recovering the successor looked only at the successor's own intents.

Reproduced first. On ae7bea8, with a broken lease store and then a broken mail store:

  • the next agent's first request had 0 tool_interrupted notes, while aos inspect still listed the call as open;
  • when the mail transfer was the one that failed, the predecessor's unread mail never reached any agent either;
  • a related, wider case: a successor whose first model call fails exits at the checkpoint it was recovered from, which never held the note. The next recovery was not told either.

The fix:

  • Recover transfers mail and leases from every process on the resumed_from chain, oldest first. A completed transfer leaves nothing behind, so it is not repeated.
  • noteInterrupted looks at the intents of the whole chain that no outcome answers, other than tool_interrupted.

How a call is never reported twice:

  • One record per intent: the first note creates the tool_interrupted record. A later note finds it by its intent metadata and reuses it.
  • One message per history: the message is added only if the session being continued does not already hold that record in ToolArtifactIDs, which only grows. A successor that checkpointed the note carries it, and is not told again.
  • Resume: keeps the PID. Its chain is the process and its ancestors, so it follows the same rule.

4. Confirmed, Not Fixed: A Delegate in Flight

  • A delegate call records no intent.
  • A recovered parent has a new PID, so Children(newPID) is empty, and delegating again spawns a fresh child.
  • What happens to the old child depends on how the parent stopped:
    • in a live engine, abandonChildren closes it, retryably, when the parent's run ends;
    • after a crash, it stays live in the kernel until a reap.
  • Either way its checkpoint is not continued, and its open intents reach no agent, because the fresh child is not on its chain.
  • A fix would record the delegation as an intent that the recovered parent can match.
