Replay check: three Terminal-Bench trials on ca78ad9

All three trials replay identically with aos replay (exit 0), including the one Harbor killed. That trial records the signal path end to end:

  • both processes exit with terminated by signal (SIGTERM);
  • there is a final checkpoint;
  • the cut call has an outcome line;
  • no request started after the agent phase ended.

On the killed trial, the new records answer the four reconstruction questions that the 72-trial probe had found wrong or unanswerable. Before, the records could not say what stopped the run, missed its in-flight request, ended early and lost its last action. Now they answer all four correctly.

This is a check, not a measurement. It covers three trials, one of each kind.
