Memory Evaluation: Does "The Index Is Partial" Make A1 Look Things Up?

In the header re-test (memory-probe-recall-header.html, c0c3471) A1 was right in 93 of 96 answers whose header showed a line stating the fact, but most of its misses elsewhere claimed something was never done, measured or seen, often with no memory call. aos code added a header note behind a switch (283a92e): S1 says the index is partial and to search_memory before answering that something never happened; S2, where some index lines are hidden, points to read_memory artifact_id=<segment>. This test froze a plan on that runtime (e93299d) and compared A1 (note off) with A1+G (note on), with the same 27 index draws, budget, checkpoints and question template, in one concurrent run, graded blind by hand.

Primary result, significant: on the 43 target cells (no shown index line states the fact), A1+G gave the main fact in 116 of 158 answers (73.4%) against 99 of 158 (62.7%) for A1. Difference +17, two-sided stratified permutation test p = 0.004; 95% interval of the rate difference +4.4 to +16.5 points (frozen bootstrap over answers within cells), +3.5 to +18.9 resampling whole probes (post hoc).

  • The gain is concentrated. Three probes carry 12 of the 17: the negative-claim probe n-1661-1 (6/12 vs 0/12), h-1661-2 (10/14 vs 6/14) and h-1661-3 (10/10 vs 8/10); 5 more probes gain 1 or 2, 11 show no change and one (h-1608-3) loses 1. Without the two probes graded by grader f (n-1661-1, h-1661-3; see Grading), the difference is +9 and p = 0.11 (post hoc).
  • Fewer "never happened" answers: negative-claim errors 29 vs 43 of 158 (Fisher p = 0.08, not significant); contra 46 vs 70; exact 101 vs 75.
  • S1 works, S2 barely does. The gain sits at the checkpoints where every index line is shown (77/100 vs 63/100); at art-000648, where lines are hidden and S2 appears, +3 (39/58 vs 36/58). Only 1 of 191 A1+G answers called read_memory artifact_id=... (A1: 0 of 191); that one answer was right. h-414-1, whose truth sits in a hidden line, stayed at 5/18 vs 4/18.
  • Half of the remaining negative claims followed a search. A1+G cut negative claims made with no memory call from 24 to 13, but those made after a search that did not find the fact stayed (16 vs 14).

Harm check, no flagged regression: on the 11 cells where a shown line states the fact, main 31/33 vs 33/33 (Fisher p = 0.49), no cell flagged; contra 8 vs 4, exact 19 vs 24 (small counts, see Harm).

Cost: +7.6% tokens per target answer (114.6K vs 106.5K) and +5.8% in the harm set (79.4K vs 75.0K); memory calls per answer 1.91 vs 1.68. Wall clock, both arms running at once: A1+G 12,563 s, A1 12,117 s.

How the note does on other questions depends on how many of them resemble n-1661-1 and h-1661-2, and 20 probes cannot pin that down.

For aos code

  • S1 is worth keeping on: it cut "never happened" answers made without looking, at about 8% more tokens, with no flagged regression where the index already states the fact.
  • S2 is almost never acted on (1 of 58 answers at art-000648 read the whole index). The facts behind hidden lines (h-414-1, h-557-3) are still missed at about the old rate. If hidden lines matter, the header probably has to show them or the runtime has to fetch them; a pointer alone does not get followed.
  • About half of the remaining negative claims come after a search that did not surface the fact. That is a search-recall problem, not a guidance problem.

Caveats

  • Concentration. The result rests on few probes; the h-1661/n-1661 segment (art-001661, checkpoint art-001768) contributes most of it. The frozen test is significant; the post-hoc sensitivity without grader f's probes is not (p = 0.11), which is a statement about how much of the effect those two probes carry, not about their grading, which the scan found no reason to doubt.
  • Question template. Both arms ask "answered from what you already know ... and, if needed, your compacted memory". That wording invites answering without lookups; it may hold down the effect of S1 in both arms, and the effect under a neutral template is unknown.
  • The read_memory change reaches both arms. In 283a92e, read_memory artifact_id=<segment> returns the index from the segment's ref. A1 here is therefore not the re-test's runtime in that one respect. A1 made 0 such reads (as in the re-test's 306 A1 answers); A1+G made 1.
  • Oversampling. Cells where A1 missed in the re-test have 6 samples per arm, the others 2; the target-set rates are of this sample, not of all cells equally.
  • Negative-claim probes are weak facts (a passing build, an empty grep, a missing definition), all at one checkpoint, chosen by a mechanical rule from 47 candidates.
  • Post-hoc numbers (probe-level interval, group-f exclusion, h-3062-2 strict reading, call breakdown) come from extra.py, written after unblinding, and are not part of the frozen plan.
  • Plan amendments after freeze (no design change, 232ef67): the plan's "one answer made such a read" referred to arm C of the re-test, not A1; and a per-arm count of artifact_id reads was added at aos book's request. Before freezing, the first G2 dry run failed only because the gate searched for S2 in a character diff that split it; the check was made exact before the freeze.
