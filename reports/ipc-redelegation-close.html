Closing the Old Chain on Re-delegation

The user's decision, 2026-11-04. When a delegator delegates again to an agent whose last child exited retryably, with no restart policy, the engine closes that child's chain, so its senders get undelivered:. Only this case. It builds on nothing in step 4's bounds, and was committed after it.

What it costs: an operator can no longer Recover that exit. What it does not do: move the old chain's mail to the new child. This is a close, not a Transfer.
