Harbor Street Library reopened on Saturday after an eight-month renovation.
The building now has a second reading room on the upper floor, a children's
corner with low shelves, and a ramp at the main entrance so that every visitor
can come in through the same door. The reading rooms stay open until 8 pm on
weekdays. Volunteers from the neighborhood association helped move about
twelve thousand books back onto the shelves in the last week before opening.
