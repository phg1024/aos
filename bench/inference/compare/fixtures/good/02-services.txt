The renovated Harbor Street Library keeps its weekday hours until 8 pm and
adds Sunday afternoon opening from 1 pm to 5 pm. The new upper reading room
is quiet study space; group work moves to two bookable rooms next to the
children's corner. Library cards issued before the renovation still work, and
the old self-checkout machines were replaced by three new ones near the ramp.
Neighborhood volunteers, who moved most of the collection back, will run a
weekly reading hour for children on Wednesdays.
